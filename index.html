Attire


We'd love to see our family and friends dress up with us! The dress code for our wedding is garden formal.

Gentlemen, we kindly ask that you wear a suit or dress shirt with trousers and a tie.

Ladies, a midi-length or floor-length gown would be a great option.

The wedding will be in Central Florida during Spring, so it will get hot out! Feel free to wear thin, flowy layers so you can be comfortable both indoors and outdoors.

Don't be afraid to have fun with color, pattern, and texture! Dress to impress, and have fun with it in your own way!

Have a look at some ideas below for outfits that would fit nicely with our vision.

Please Keep in Mind
  • Guests with tattoos do not need to worry about covering them up.
  • Avoid wearing the shades of blue and green used in this website, as those colors are reserved for the wedding party.
