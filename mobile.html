Attire


The dress code for our wedding will be garden formal. This means wearing a suit and tie for men and a floor-length gown for ladies. The wedding will be in Central Florida during Spring, so it will get hot out! Feel free to wear thin, flowy layers so you can be comfortable both indoors and outdoors.

Have a look at some ideas below for guidelines on the range of outfits you can express yourself with!

Guests with tattoos do not need to worry about covering them up.
